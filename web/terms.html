Home Dashboard – Terms and Conditions

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Home Dashboard (“the Application”) is a private, non-commercial smart home dashboard operated by its owner for personal and household use. It shows the status of, and lets authorized users control, the owner’s own connected devices, including Tuya / Smart Life devices, Google Nest devices, devices managed through Home Assistant, and the owner’s Volvo vehicle.

1. Who may use the Application

  • Access is limited to the owner and household members whose accounts the owner has explicitly added to an allowlist. The Application is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
  • Each user may only connect accounts, vehicles and devices that they own or are authorized to use.

2. Third-party services

The Application connects to third-party services (such as Volvo Cars, Google, Tuya and Home Assistant) only after the account holder signs in to that service and grants consent. Use of those services remains subject to their own terms. The Application is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Volvo Cars, Google, Tuya or Home Assistant.

3. Data we access and why

For a connected Volvo vehicle, the Application may read: lock and door status, battery level or fuel level, electric range, odometer, engine status and vehicle location. It may also send the remote commands the user selects: lock, unlock, start or stop climate, and honk and flash. For smart home devices it reads device names, on/off state, brightness, temperature, humidity and similar status, and sends the commands the user selects.

This data is used only to display current status in the dashboard and to carry out commands requested by an authorized user.

4. How data is stored and protected

  • Only the latest device and vehicle status is stored, plus a log of commands sent (who sent them, what, when and whether they succeeded).
  • Data is stored in the owner’s private Google Firebase project, encrypted at rest and readable only by allowlisted users.
  • Access tokens and API credentials are stored server-side only and are never sent to the browser.
  • Status is refreshed when the dashboard is used and periodically (about every 15 minutes).

5. Sharing

Data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and is never used for advertising, profiling or analytics. Data is only sent to the third-party service it belongs to, for example a lock command is sent to Volvo Cars.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

A user can disconnect any integration at any time from the Integrations page. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens, credentials and device data for that service. Consent can also be revoked directly with the third-party provider, for example in the user’s Volvo ID or Google account settings. The command log can be deleted on request.

7. Safety and no warranty

Remote commands depend on third-party services and network connectivity and may be delayed or fail. Users should not rely on the Application for safety-critical purposes and should confirm important actions, such as locking a vehicle or door, in the manufacturer’s official app. The Application is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind, to the extent permitted by law.
